PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FinanceAI handles information provided by users while using the financial analytics platform.

Last updated: September 2026

01 About FinanceAI

FinanceAI is an educational financial analytics platform developed using Python, Flask and machine-learning techniques. It provides personal finance analysis, credit stress insights and experimental loan risk analysis.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, FinanceAI may collect information such as:

  • Name and account information.
  • Email address used during registration.
  • Financial values entered for analysis.
  • Income, expenses, savings and debt-related information.
  • Credit or loan-related data entered by the user.
  • Analysis history generated inside the platform.

03 How Information Is Used

Information entered into FinanceAI is used to provide financial-health analysis and other platform features.

  • Generate financial health scores.
  • Provide personalized educational recommendations.
  • Generate experimental credit stress insights.
  • Generate experimental loan risk signals.
  • Maintain account and analysis history.
  • Improve application functionality and reliability.

04 Financial Information

FinanceAI may process financial information entered voluntarily by users. Users should avoid entering highly sensitive financial credentials such as banking passwords, ATM PINs, card CVVs, UPI PINs or similar authentication information.

05 Machine Learning

Some FinanceAI modules use experimental machine-learning models for demonstration and educational purposes.

Machine-learning outputs should not be treated as guaranteed predictions, credit decisions or professional financial advice.

06 Data Security

FinanceAI uses reasonable technical measures to protect application data and user accounts. However, no web application or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

07 Password Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials and should use strong and unique passwords.

08 Third-Party Services

FinanceAI may use third-party libraries, frameworks or hosting infrastructure. Those services may operate according to their own privacy policies.

09 Educational Purpose

FinanceAI is primarily an academic and educational project. Information generated by the platform should not replace consultation with qualified financial, legal or lending professionals.

10 Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FinanceAI features, technologies or data practices change. Updated information will be published on this page.

11 Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the FinanceAI project, contact the project team.
