ABOUT FINANCEAI 2.0

Intelligent Personal Finance & Risk Analysis System

FinanceAI is a Flask-based personal finance platform that combines money management, explainable analytics, machine learning and a local retrieval-based finance assistant in one application.

What is FinanceAI?

FinanceAI helps users understand their financial condition by tracking income, expenses, savings, budgets, goals, assets, liabilities and other financial indicators.

It converts financial data into readable scores, forecasts, alerts, spending insights and educational recommendations.

Project Objective

The objective is to provide simple and explainable financial insights while demonstrating Flask, databases, data analytics, machine learning and local information retrieval in one project.

FinanceAI is designed as an educational decision-support system, not as a replacement for professional financial or lending advice.

CORE MODULES

FinanceAI Modules

01

Financial Health

Generates a 0–100 Financial Health Score with prioritized educational recommendations.

02

Transactions & Budget

Tracks income and expenses, category budgets, CSV data and a 50/30/20 planning baseline.

03

Goals & Net Worth

Tracks savings goals, assets, investments and liabilities to show overall financial progress.

04

Cash-flow Forecast

Uses transaction history to estimate income, expenses and savings over 30, 60 and 90 days.

05

Spending Intelligence

Uses K-Means for spending behavior and Isolation Forest to flag unusual spending patterns.

06

Credit Stress

Uses a trained Logistic Regression model for experimental financial-stress analysis.

07

Loan Risk

Uses an Extra Trees model for experimental historical loan-risk ranking and analysis.

08

Local Finance Assistant

Uses local TF-IDF retrieval and FinanceAI account context to explain finance concepts without an external AI API.

09

History & Reports

Stores analyses in readable tables and supports CSV exports and downloadable PDF reports.
